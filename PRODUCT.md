# Product

Nool, the Tamil word for thread (நூல்), warns a garment exporter early when an order is running late. Supervisors post daily floor numbers from their phones, the risk engine compares them with the plan, and buyers see progress through a private read-only link.

## People

| Who | Where | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Merchandiser | Desktop, bright office | See which orders need attention, set up orders, confirm what buyers are told |
| Supervisor | Phone with 2-3 GB RAM, factory floor, patchy network | Enter today's count in Tamil, with or without signal |
| Buyer | UK and EU, any device | See their own orders' progress, and nothing else |

## Rules the interface must keep

- One buyer never sees another buyer's styles or volumes.
- A warning reaches a buyer only after a merchandiser confirms it.
- A note's original text always sits beside its translation, and its category stays editable.
- A supervisor's update is never lost to a bad network.
- Every date change keeps its reason and history.

## Out of scope

Costing, invoicing, export documents, fabric inventory, attendance and payroll.
